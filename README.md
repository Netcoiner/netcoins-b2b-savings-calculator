# Netcoins Business Savings Calculator

A single-page, dependency-free calculator that estimates what a business could save by moving part of its payments onto crypto rails:

- **Customer payments**: card processing fees and chargebacks vs. crypto processing + network fees
- **International payments**: bank wire fees and FX markup vs. stablecoin transfers
- **Settlement**: cash held in card settlement that would arrive sooner (shown, not counted as savings)

Three example profiles (online retailer, importer/wholesaler, agency) and every fee assumption are editable.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or view it on GitHub Pages.

> For illustration only. Figures are estimates based on user-entered assumptions and are not quotes, financial, tax or legal advice. Customer-facing use requires compliance review.
