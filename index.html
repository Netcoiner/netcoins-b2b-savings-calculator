Talk to Netcoins

Lower processing fees

Card networks take a percentage plus a fixed fee on every sale. Crypto payments usually cost a flat processing and conversion rate, with no interchange.

No forced chargebacks

Confirmed blockchain payments cannot be reversed by a third party. You still handle refunds, on your own terms, without dispute fees.

Cheaper cross-border transfers

Stablecoin transfers skip correspondent banks and wide FX markups, and arrive in minutes instead of two to five business days.

For illustration only. Figures are estimates based on the assumptions entered above and example profiles. They are not quotes and do not describe any specific Netcoins product or fee. Actual costs depend on your providers, payment networks, volumes and market conditions.

Crypto asset prices can move quickly. Converting incoming payments to CAD on receipt, or using stablecoins, reduces but does not remove that exposure. Stablecoins carry their own issuer and liquidity risks. Some customers and suppliers will not use crypto.
